# Member Achievements & Competitions
Competition results, awards, and recognitions earned by student members across national and international robotics competitions.

### 3rd Place — RoboCup 2026 (Middle Size League) (2026)
Members: Muhammad Navis Azka Atqiya, Abrar Rafi Dwianto, Yuke Brilliant Hestiavin
Details: Earned 3rd place in Robot Soccer – Middle Size League at RoboCup Incheon 2026, South Korea, as members of IRIS ITS. The team competed with autonomous soccer robots combining computer vision, motion planning, and real-time decision-making.

### Best Rookie Team — Australian Rover Challenge (2026)
Members: Moh. Wildan Risqi Maulidi
Details: RIVAL ITS earned the Best Rookie Team award and finished 6th among 18 international teams at the Australian Rover Challenge 2026. Its AEROVAL semi-autonomous rover competed in simulated lunar exploration missions involving resource exploration, excavation, construction, and autonomous mapping.
