# B401 Robotics & Intelligent Systems Laboratory
Department of Computer Engineering, Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), Surabaya.
Overview: The Robotics and Intelligent Systems Laboratory (Laboratorium Robotika dan Sistem Cerdas) is a core research and academic facility within the Department of Computer Engineering at ITS, Surabaya. The laboratory provides a comprehensive environment for undergraduate and postgraduate students to study, design, and innovate across the fields of hardware software integration, autonomous systems, and modern connectivity.   The lab is heavily utilized for academic practicums, collaborative student lecturer research, and the execution of final year projects and master's theses. It is a hub for developing cutting edge solutions bridging the physical world with intelligent algorithms.
Mission & Focus: Research, practicums, final year projects, hardware-software integration, autonomous systems, and embedded connectivity.
