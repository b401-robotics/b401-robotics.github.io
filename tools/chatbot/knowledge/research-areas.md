# Research Areas & Expertise
Our interdisciplinary research spans multiple domains, bringing together expertise in robotics, AI, and systems engineering.
Key Domains:
- Web Development (Web): Research and development of modern web based applications and platforms.
- Embedded Systems (Embedded): Design and implementation of embedded hardware and software for various applications.
- Robotics (Robotics): Development of robotic systems, kinematics, and intelligent control algorithms.
- Wireless Sensor Networks (Network): Wireless sensor networks for environmental monitoring and large scale data collection.
- App Development (App): Mobile and desktop application development for intelligent systems integration.
- Internet of Things (IoT): Integration of physical devices with the internet to build interconnected smart systems.
